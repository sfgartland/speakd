Prototype against a simulated event stream. The scrubber is the span-to-time map: each tick's width is that segment's real duration, so the bar shows the utterance's rhythm rather than a uniform progress bar. Clicking a sentence or a tick sends seek, resolved through Timeline.time_of. Drift is the gap between the nominal audio clock and wall time — it grows whenever synthesis stalls playback, which is why the timeline carries two clocks rather than one.
